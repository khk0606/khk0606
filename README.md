## The question behind my work

> A chair tells us where sitting is possible. What tells us how to approach it, when to sit, and what to do next?

That question connects my interests in **human–scene interaction**, **affordance learning**, and **motion generation**. I am working toward representations that describe an interaction in context, rather than an isolated action.

## On my workbench

### 01 / Making motion aware of its scene

**[ADM-MoE-Teacher →](https://github.com/khk0606/ADM-MoE-Teacher)**

An ongoing research pipeline for scene-conditioned human motion: teacher adaptation, mixture-of-experts routing, and contact supervision. My work includes motion-data conversion, spatial alignment checks, and experiments on what interaction representations lose when pooled over time.

**Current emphasis:** validating the data and evaluation pipeline; end-to-end performance is still under evaluation.

### 02 / Turning a video into a control problem

**[Video-to-Robot Motion Transfer →](https://github.com/khk0606/Zero-Shot-Video-to-Robot-Motion-Transfer)**

A simulation prototype connecting reference-video interpretation to motion specifications, reward functions, and Dial-MPC control of a quadruped in MuJoCo/MJX.

### 03 / Testing what survives text distortion

**[Korean Text Robustness →](https://github.com/khk0606/Korean-Hate-Speech-Robustness-under-Text-Obfuscation)**

An evaluation of Korean hate-speech classifiers under character obfuscation, examining how normalization and calibration change false-positive and false-negative trade-offs.

A smaller experiment: **[Chair Pose](https://github.com/khk0606/chair_pose)** — plausible 2D sitting keypoints from a chair-only image.
